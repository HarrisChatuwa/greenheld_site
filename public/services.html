Our Comprehensive Research & Consulting Services

Greenheld offers a wide array of social research and consulting services designed to meet the unique needs of NGOs, government agencies, and private sector organizations. We are committed to delivering actionable insights and strategic guidance to foster social impact.

Social and Health Research

We specialize in conducting baseline, mid-term and end-line surveys to support evidence based decision making. This provides accountability and learning through comprehensive research methodologies.

Key Offerings:

  • Quantitative and qualitative survey designs, study protocols and tools development
  • Sampling techniques (random, stratified, cluster)
  • Training and deploying research assistants for data collection
  • Data transcription and translation
  • Rigorous data cleaning, analysis and reporting

Capacity Building and Training

We are committed to strengthening the capabilities of organizations and individuals through customized training programs and workshops designed to be practical, participatory, and relevant to specific client needs.

Key Offerings:

  • Customized training workshops on research methodology (qualitative and quantitative)
  • Training on M & E principles and framework designs
  • Training on data collection techniques and tools (ODK, Kobo toolbox, Survey CTO)
  • Training on data analysis software (R, SPSS, STATA, Python, Excel, NVivo)
  • Training on qualitative data transcription and translation
  • Coaching and mentorship for research and M & E teams
